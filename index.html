About Our Project

Smart Crop Rotation Management System is a web-based application designed to help farmers plan crop cultivation using crop rotation methods.


Project Objectives

1. To provide information about different crops.
2. To suggest suitable crops after previous cultivation.
3. To provide seasonal crop planning.
4. To create awareness about soil fertility.
5. To encourage sustainable agriculture.
